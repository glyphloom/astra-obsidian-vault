## The Part Nobody Mentions at Dinner

Diachronic Security is Archive's armed division. It holds a licence to use force, issued three times, by three governments, in three centuries. Archive later made sure each of those governments had existed.

It is not a secret. It is just not discussed, the way nobody discusses what the building's foundations are made of.
