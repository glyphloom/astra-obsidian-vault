## The Entry

> [!quote] Parish notary of Varda, 14 March 1683, as transcribed by Archive
> Received of Jan Oost, salt merchant, two silver for the writing down that his son Pieter, aged nine, lived in this parish, was baptised here, and was drowned in the brine pans on the second of this month. Witnessed by the undersigned, who knew the boy.

That is the whole thing. Two silver to make sure that a nine-year-old stayed real.

## Why It Matters

Every Archive plan is this entry at scale. The first contract template copies its wording. The 1683 founding year, which appeared in every record on [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-22|Tuesday]] and in everyone's memory except mine, points here. In provenance terms the entry is an *attractor*: so many independent paths lead back to it that history keeps rebuilding it.

It is also inside the [[Clean Cut]] zone, which is the problem.
