## What This Is

A witness structure is anything that keeps a person in history: a record, a ledger, somebody who remembers. Archive sells them. This note is one I give away.

Every name here belongs to someone nobody is paying to remember. I write them down, link them to where I found them, and keep this vault backed up in three places. It is not much causal armor. It is more than nothing, and it is outside the [[Clean Cut]] zone, which is the only place armor still works after an excision.

Nothing in my contract forbids keeping a diary. I checked twice.
