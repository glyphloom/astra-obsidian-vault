## 212 Pages, One a Night

The Varda parish register covers baptisms, marriages, and burials from 1671 to 1688. Every person who lived on the salt flats is in it somewhere. When [[Clean Cut]] goes through, the register goes with the zone, and so does everyone in it.

I am copying it, one page a night, into this vault, which lives outside the zone. It is slow. It is the only externalized armor these people have.
