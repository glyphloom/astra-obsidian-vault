## The Recipe on the Canteen Wall

Served every Thursday since, according to everyone including me, the canteen opened. According to my notes, since 22 September 2026.

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Salted herring fillets | 12 |
| Red onion, sliced thin | 2 |
| Apple, grated | 1 |
| Sour cream | 300 g |
| Dill | A handful |
| Black pepper | "Until someone objects" |

1. Soak the fillets in milk for an hour, because the salt is from Varda and Varda salt is strong.
2. Fold everything else together and season it.
3. Lay the fillets on top and serve cold, with boiled potatoes, to people who did not ask for it.

## Provenance

The card says the recipe is "after the Varda way, 1683". That is the same year, and the same place, as [[Ledger Zero]]. Whatever is reaching forward from that page brought lunch with it.
