### What It Is

*Memento* is a 2000 thriller written and directed by Christopher Nolan, from a story by Jonathan Nolan. Leonard Shelby, who cannot form new memories, hunts his wife's killer using notes, Polaroid photographs, and tattoos. Its colour scenes run in reverse order and alternate with black-and-white scenes that run forward, until the two meet ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memento_(film))).

### Why It Belongs Here

The film is about external memory as evidence, and about how easily a record can be curated into a story. That is the everyday work of [[Archive]], viewed from the customer's side.
