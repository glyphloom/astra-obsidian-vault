## Night Shift Soundtrack

The only album that makes drawing maps of places scheduled for deletion feel honest. The loops are falling apart while you listen, and you can hear exactly where.

- Best on the balcony, with the register open.
- Side one, track one, until the kettle gives up.
- Do not play it at work. Somebody on the floor will ask what it is and you will have to explain.

### What It Is

*The Disintegration Loops* is a series of albums by William Basinski, released from 2002 to 2003. In 2001 he tried to digitise tape loops he had recorded in the early 1980s and found that the aged tape was crumbling as it passed the playhead, so each loop slowly decays over the course of a track. He finished the recordings around 11 September 2001 in New York, and the albums became associated with that day ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Disintegration_Loops)).

### Why It Belongs Here

It is a record of a record being lost, which is the [[Varda Parish Register]] problem set to music, and a companion to [[Sorting Is Never Free]].
