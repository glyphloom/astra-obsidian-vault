### What It Is

"Funes the Memorious" is a short story by Jorge Luis Borges, first published in 1942 and collected in *Ficciones* (1944). The narrator recalls Ireneo Funes, a young man in Uruguay who, after a fall from a horse, remembers every detail of everything he perceives and can no longer generalise or sleep easily ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funes_the_Memorious)).

### Why It Belongs Here

Funes is a perfect witness structure in one body, and the story's point is that total recall is not understanding. It pairs with [[Forgetting Is a Feature]] and with the [[Quiet Ledger]], which chooses what to keep instead of keeping everything.
