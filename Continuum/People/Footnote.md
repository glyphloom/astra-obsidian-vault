## Key Interactions & Notes

- An [[Errata|erratum]]. Found on [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-25#The Drawer|Friday]] in my top desk drawer, fat and asleep, on top of the folder where Tomas's expense claims used to be.
- Its wings say `Brandt, T. — Procurement — ext. 4417`. It ate the last paper record of [[Tomas Brandt]] some time after Friday the 18th. As far as I know, it is now the only object in the world that spells his name.
- Lives in a jam jar on my desk with a slice of blank paper, which it ignores. It only eats what history has abandoned, and blank paper has no history at all.
- The print is fading. By Sunday the extension number was gone. I photographed the wings every morning so the name at least exists twice.
- Facilities would spray it. I have told nobody. It is in the [[Quiet Ledger]], because it is a witness now, whether it likes it or not.

> [!question] Open question
> If I let it go, does it forget Tomas too? Or has it already?
