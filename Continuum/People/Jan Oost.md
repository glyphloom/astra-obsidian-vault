## Key Interactions & Notes

- Salt merchant in Varda, 1683. Paid two silver to have it written down that his son Pieter existed: [[Ledger Zero]].
- Also paid for a pew, on page seven of the [[Varda Parish Register]]. So before he was a founding story he was a man who wanted somewhere to sit on Sundays.
- Archive's training deck calls him "our first customer". He did not buy a plan. He bought one sentence, once, because he loved his son.
- Lives inside the [[Clean Cut]] zone, which means that in eleven weeks he may never have lived at all.
