## Key Interactions & Notes

- Procurement, fourth floor, the desk by the window that is now a printer.
- Took his coffee with too much sugar and apologised for it every time.
- Lent me an umbrella in August. It is still in my hallway, which makes it the only object I know with [[Black Provenance|black provenance]]: here, owned by nobody, and from nowhere.
- Last seen on Friday the 18th. On [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-21|Monday]] his desk was a printer, his badge number belonged to a fire door, and his manager had never had a fourth report.
- I remember him and there is no reason I should. No records, no photos, no plan. The rewrite reached everyone's memory but somehow not mine.
- Photographed the umbrella on [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-25#The Umbrella|Friday]], in case it goes too.

- On [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-25#The Drawer|Friday]] I found the [[Errata|erratum]] that ate his last paper record. His name is printed on its wings. I call it [[Footnote]].

He is the first entry in the [[Quiet Ledger]].
