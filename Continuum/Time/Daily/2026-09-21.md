## Notes

### Tomas Is Gone

Went to give [[Tomas Brandt|Tomas]] his umbrella back. His desk is a printer. Not a new printer: a printer with a year of coffee rings on it. His manager was polite about it and said she has always had three reports. HR has never issued his badge number to a person; it opens a fire door on the sixth floor.

I remember him. That is the part that bothers me. A rewrite is supposed to take your memories along with everything else, so the new past feels like yours. Either this one missed me, or I am the one who is wrong.

The umbrella is still in my hallway.

### Evening

Rewatched [[Memento]], which was a mistake. A man keeps Polaroids because he can't keep memories, and I spent the whole film wondering whether my notes about Tomas are a Polaroid or a tattoo.
