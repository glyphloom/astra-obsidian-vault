### Herring Thursday

It was herring. It has always been herring. The canteen staff were kind about my question and looked at me the way you look at someone who has asked what colour the sky is. The recipe is on the wall by the till and I copied it into [[Thursday Herring]], because a recipe is a record and records are what I have.

The cat was under the till again. Staff call it [[Receipt]].

### Diachronic Security Came to the Fourth Floor

Two of them, in the grey suits that are not uniforms. They wanted every map of Varda printed before the Orrery run finishes "in case the maps stop agreeing". [[Sergeant Dace]] did the talking. He was polite, took his coffee black, and asked whether anyone on the floor keeps personal notes about the zone.

Nobody said anything. I didn't either.
