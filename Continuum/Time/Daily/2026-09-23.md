### Maxwell's Demon on the Records Floor

Watched the sorting clerks on the second floor route fresh records left and stale ones right, all day, for a building that sells order. They are Maxwell's demon with a lanyard. The cost of sorting doesn't vanish; it goes into the filing system, and the filing system is us. Wrote it up as [[Sorting Is Never Free]].

### The Balcony

I watered the plant. It seemed only fair.
