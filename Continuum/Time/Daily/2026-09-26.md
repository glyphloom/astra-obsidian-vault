## Notes

### A Slow Saturday

Six pages of the register on the balcony, with tea. The names are ordinary: Oost, Brouwer, Kaan, three different women called Aaltje. One family has nine children baptised in eleven years and not one of them buried. I stopped for a while after that page.

[[Jan Oost]] appears on page seven, paying for a pew. So he was a real person before he was a founding story. That seems important and I don't know why yet.

### The Clock

The kitchen clock was four minutes fast at breakfast and two minutes slow at lunch, and nobody touched it. The [[Rot Tier Field Card|field card]] says that is Tier 0 rot noise and to drink water. I drank water. The clock is now right, which is worse.

### The Sergeant's Question

Thought about Thursday all afternoon. Keeping notes is not against any rule. Keeping notes about a zone that is about to stop having existed might be the only thing that is. I backed the vault up twice.
