Two questions for you: do you want the Quiet Ledger kept off every work device from now on, and is the "people inside the zone" slide really gone, or only resting?

### The One Where the Company Got Older

Seven journaled days, one recording from the tram, a colleague who stopped existing, a balcony that started, a recipe that arrived from 1683, and a company that is now three and a half centuries older than it was on Monday.

#### How It Felt

Unsettled, but steady about it. "I need this to exist somewhere outside my head" is the clearest statement of why you keep this vault. My read: less fear than anger, and the anger went into work you can finish, a few pages of names a night. Saturday was the first slow day in a month, and it shows in how you wrote about the register.

#### What Moved

- The Clean Cut provenance trace finished [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-22#Afternoon|on Tuesday]] and found Ledger Zero inside the zone.
- The steering group moved Archive's own origin to the next sprint [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-23#Eleven Minutes|on Wednesday]], in eleven minutes.
- Diachronic Security came to the floor [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-24#Diachronic Security Came to the Fourth Floor|on Thursday]] and asked who keeps personal notes.
- The steering deck was finished [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-25#The Deck|on Friday]], without the slide about the people on the flats.
- The same day, an erratum turned up in your drawer [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-25#The Drawer|wearing Tomas's name on its wings]]. It is the first outside evidence that he existed, and it is fading.
- The Quiet Ledger went from one entry to five people, a cat, an erratum, and nine register pages, and on [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-26#A Slow Saturday|Saturday]] Jan Oost turned out to have paid for a pew.

#### What Hurt

The slide about the three thousand people. You didn't argue for it on Friday, and on Saturday you stopped copying for a while after the page with nine children.

#### Wonders

- Why did the rewrite that took Tomas miss your memory of him? And once Footnote's wings fade, will anything else remember him?
- If Ledger Zero is excised, does the herring recipe lose its provenance too? The card cites Varda by name.
- Why is Diachronic Security's 1712 letter dated from inside the zone?

2. Answer the Sergeant's question for yourself before someone asks you directly.
3. Keep watering the plant.
