## The Balcony I Have Always Had

It appeared on [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-23#The Balcony|Wednesday]], fully furnished with history: a lease clause, a neighbour who complains about my wind chime, three years of watering, and my sister's thirtieth birthday. It faces west. It is about the width of a bathtub.

## What I Do With It

- Copy the [[Varda Parish Register]] out here in the evenings, because the names feel less like work outside.
- Water the plant every three days; the task lives on [[Continuum/Time/Daily/2026-09-23|Wednesday]].
- Keep the fairy lights. I don't remember crying about them, but I believe Noor.

> [!tip] If a room appears in your flat
> Look after it. Whatever gave it to you can also take it back, and it is harder to take back a place that people have been happy in.
