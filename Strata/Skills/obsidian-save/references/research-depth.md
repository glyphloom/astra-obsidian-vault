# Research and connection depth

Read this reference for durable media, knowledge, people, movements, places, technologies, and other subjects that warrant a standalone note. Depth follows the subject, not the amount of pixels or text in the capture.

## Depth ladder

- **Fleeting capture:** Preserve the user's text and source through the daily flow. Add no survey research.
- **Terse list item:** Record enough identity and one useful reason to recognize it later. Do not manufacture a standalone article.
- **Durable subject:** Aim for roughly 500–1,000 useful words across relevant sections, three to six strong sources, and three to seven defensible vault connections when the material supports them. These are quality targets, not padding quotas; for a subject selected from the owner's daily reflections, narrow the research to what helps understand their lived perspective rather than letting the target length dominate the note.
- **Steered or intrinsically substantial subject:** Follow the user's requested depth without a fixed ceiling. Prefer coherent synthesis over accumulating excerpts.

### Requested related-work atoms

When the owner explicitly asks for each referenced work as a separate atomic note, each durable item needs a real account, not a thumbnail plus a mention. Target at least 450 useful agent-owned words, `### Abstract`, `### History`, and one or more relevant H3 sections, backed by a primary/canonical source plus contextual evidence where available. Explain what the work is, what it does or contains, and why its relation to the parent matters. A thinner result is acceptable only when the source record itself is genuinely sparse; state that limitation plainly and retain the research basis in the receipt.

An unattended four-minute save may land below the durable target when sources are difficult, but it must go materially beyond resolver metadata. Preserve the strongest facts and relationships already established rather than filling space with generic prose.

## Source strategy

Use a small, complementary source set:

1. A primary or canonical source for identity and first-party facts: the work itself, creator, publisher, label, studio, registry, paper, specification, or official documentation.
2. A reliable contextual source for history, interpretation, reception, implementation, or controversy.
3. A relational source when needed to establish an influence, lineage, comparison, movement, application, or dispute.

Add more sources only when they contribute a distinct function. Search-result snippets, scraped aggregators, and pages repeating the same synopsis do not create depth. Never imply full access to a book, film, paper, transcript, or recording that was not actually inspected.

## Subject structures

Always retain `### Abstract` and `### History` for a durable standalone subject. Choose additional sections by what the subject needs.

For media, consider:

- `### Production` for creators, circumstances, release, and material choices.
- `### Form and Themes` for structure, style, technique, recurring ideas, and aesthetic effect.
- `### Reception and Legacy` for contemporary response, later influence, reinterpretation, and durable significance.
- `### Related Works` only when comparison warrants sustained treatment beyond links integrated elsewhere.

For knowledge, consider:

- `### Core Ideas` for definitions, claims, and conceptual components.
- `### Mechanism` for how the system, method, argument, or phenomenon works.
- `### Applications` for concrete uses and consequences.
- `### Debates and Limits` for assumptions, counterarguments, failure modes, uncertainty, and scope.

Do not force every heading into every note. Avoid plot dumps, encyclopedia lead rewrites, review-score inventories, and generic praise. Explain the substance that will help the owner remember, reason, and make connections later.

## Connection pass

Research first, then construct a compact relationship map using only supported axes:

- creator, collaborator, institution, place, and historical moment;
- movement, genre, school, lineage, predecessor, successor, and influence;
- technique, mechanism, formal device, technology, and material constraint;
- theme, concept, problem, application, dispute, and limitation;
- project use, creative reference, or explicit personal context supplied by the owner.

Probe the vault separately for exact entities and for the strongest conceptual relationships. Read each plausible candidate in context before linking it. Prefer a few structurally useful links over dense accidental graphing.

Integrate each wiki-link into the section where its relationship explains the subject: history for lineage, production for collaborators, mechanism for technical dependencies, themes for conceptual parallels, and so on. State the relationship in the surrounding sentence. Do not create a standalone `### Connections` section unless the owner requests a relationship index. Existing notes are preferred. Add at most one to three intentional unresolved wiki-links when the missing subject is stable, central to the researched relationship, and plausibly deserves a future note; declare them to validation and the receipt. Do not create redlinks for incidental names.

Put descriptive Markdown source links inline beside the claims they support. Do not create a default `### Sources` section; use a separate bibliography only when citation density, formal scholarship, or the owner's request makes it materially clearer. Preserve the full source-role audit in the save receipt.

Where useful, connect in both directions only when updating the related note would add durable meaning and remain within authorship boundaries. Never mutate another note merely to inflate backlink count.

## Four-minute allocation

For unattended saves, use this as a bias rather than a timer ritual:

- Resolve, deduplicate, and route: about 35 seconds.
- Source research and synthesis: about 95 seconds.
- Concept extraction and vault connection pass: about 50 seconds.
- Representative image and banner: about 20 seconds.
- Draft, transaction, validation, ledger, and receipt: reserve about 40 seconds.

When a source is slow or blocked, move on. Reduce source breadth or image sophistication before sacrificing the connection pass or final verification.
