## Plugin Settings

This vault uses 46 community plugins and the Minimal theme. Five plugins come with it; the first time you open the vault, the Public Vault plugin offers to install the others and the theme from the community directory. Their settings are copied from the original vault, leaving out anything private such as tokens, file history, and personal notes.
