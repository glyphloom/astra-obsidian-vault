## A Perfect Record Is Not a Mind

[[Funes the Memorious|Funes]] remembers everything and cannot think, because thinking means letting differences go. A record that keeps everything is not a memory. It is a warehouse.

Archive sells the warehouse. What people actually want from being remembered is smaller and harder: one person who would notice if they were gone. That is why [[A Record Is a Vote for a Past|records work like votes]] and not like truth. A witness is valuable because they chose what to keep.

The [[Quiet Ledger]] tries to be that kind of memory. It keeps a name, where it was found, and one line about why. It does not try to keep everything, and it is allowed to be wrong.
