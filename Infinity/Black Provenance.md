## Continuity Without an Origin

```card
id: black-provenance
q: What is black provenance?
a: "Continuity without a recoverable origin: something persists after its supporting history has been excised or hidden."
topic: chronodaemonia/provenance
created: 2026-09-22
```
Black provenance is what is left when something keeps existing after the history that explains it has been cut away. It is still here. Nobody can prove where it came from.

It sounds exotic and is actually everywhere once you look: an umbrella in a hallway that belongs to [[Tomas Brandt|nobody]], a [[Thursday Herring|recipe card]] that cites a place about to stop having existed, a company founded on a page that is about to be [[Ledger Zero|excised]].

```card
id: excision-rerouting
q: When a region is excised, how does surrounding history continue?
a: By excision rerouting, finding the shortest compatible continuation, which can preserve continuity while destroying proof of origin.
topic: chronodaemonia/provenance
created: 2026-09-22
```
History does not leave a hole where an excised region was. It routes around it by the shortest path that still makes sense. That is why black provenance is survivable: the world keeps going, it just stops being able to explain itself.

```card
id: externalized-admissibility
q: Why is externalized admissibility the main way to survive an excision?
a: Causal armor inside the excised region stops working, but support placed outside it lets surrounding history preserve continuity.
topic: chronodaemonia/provenance
created: 2026-09-22
```
The only defence is to put evidence outside the region before the cut. The field calls that externalized admissibility. I call it the [[Quiet Ledger]].

The armor is outside. That is the whole trick.
